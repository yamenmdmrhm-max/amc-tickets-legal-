This Privacy Policy explains how the AMC Tickets Discord application (“AMC Tickets”, “Bot”, “we”, “us”) handles information when people use its ticket features in Discord.

Important: AMC Tickets stores ticket messages and ticket content in an SQLite database. Ticket content may contain personal information that users choose to share.

1. Information we collect

  • Ticket content: messages and other text submitted as part of a ticket, including messages from users and staff.
  • Discord identifiers: user IDs and identifiers associated with tickets or the Discord server, where needed to create, manage, and associate ticket records.
  • Ticket-management information: information needed to operate the ticket system, such as ticket status and related records.

The Bot is not intended to collect passwords, payment-card information, government identification numbers, or other highly sensitive information. Please do not submit such information in tickets.

2. How we use information

We use this information only to provide and maintain ticket functionality, let authorized server staff review and respond to tickets, preserve ticket records as configured, troubleshoot issues, and protect the Bot and its users.

3. Database storage and access

Ticket information is stored in an SQLite database used by the Bot. Access should be limited to the Bot operator and server personnel who are authorized to administer or review tickets. Server owners should configure Discord channel and role permissions carefully, because staff with access to a ticket may be able to read its contents.

4. Sharing

We do not sell ticket content or personal information, and we do not use it for advertising. Information may be processed by Discord as part of Discord’s own services, and may be accessible to authorized server staff. It may also be disclosed if required by law or when reasonably necessary to protect users, the service, or legal rights.

5. Retention and deletion

Ticket records are kept in the SQLite database for as long as they are needed to provide the ticket service and according to the Bot operator’s retention and deletion settings. When a ticket is deleted through the Bot or by an administrator, associated database records should also be deleted where the Bot’s implementation supports that action. Backups, if any, may retain copies for a limited period before they are overwritten.

To request access to or deletion of information associated with a ticket, contact the AMC Tickets operator using the contact details below. Include enough information to identify the ticket, but do not send passwords or other sensitive credentials. Requests may be subject to applicable legal requirements and reasonable identity checks.

6. Security

We use the protections available in the Bot’s hosting and database environment to help prevent unauthorized access. No storage or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure. The Bot operator should restrict access to the database file and its backups.

7. Children and age requirements

AMC Tickets is provided for use within Discord and is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children below the minimum age permitted to use Discord in their location. Users should follow Discord’s age requirements and applicable local law.

8. Changes to this Policy

This Policy may be updated when the Bot’s features or data practices change. The “Last updated” date above will be revised when changes are made. Continued use of the Bot after an update means the updated Policy applies.
